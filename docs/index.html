Codes and activation links.

CodeSnap detects OTP fields and "Check your email" confirmation screens, finds matching codes (483 921, AB3-9K2, HTML emails) or safe HTTPS activation links, and fills or opens them only when you click.

12+ mail services, zero servers.

Gmail Web works from an open mail tab. CodeSnap Pro also offers optional direct Gmail OAuth, subject to Google authorization. Yandex, Outlook, Yahoo, Mail.ru, Proton, iCloud, Zoho, Rambler, Fastmail, AOL and GMX work locally via open webmail tabs in Chrome (plus optional local JMAP/Graph APIs for Fastmail and Outlook).

Built-in 2FA authenticator

Generate TOTP codes in the browser, import your accounts, and protect the vault with a PIN or passphrase. CodeSnap Pro removes the five-account limit.

Privacy

Everything runs locally inside your browser. No CodeSnap servers, no Python helpers, no stored email passwords, no ads or analytics. Read details in our privacy policy.
