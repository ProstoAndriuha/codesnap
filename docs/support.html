CodeSnap help

Gmail

Connect through the popup and authorize read-only Gmail access. Testing-mode access may require your address to be added to the Google test-user list. Extra accounts use temporary sessions and may need reconnecting.

Mail through CodeSnap Cloud

Gmail — напрямую через Google. Яндекс — официальный OAuth и CodeSnap Cloud. Yahoo, Mail.ru и IMAP — пароль приложения через Cloud. Установка дополнительных программ не нужна. Данные подключения шифруются на сервере; письма не сохраняются на диск.

Unknown request after updating

Reload CodeSnap on chrome://extensions and reopen its popup. The browser may still be running the previous service worker after files have been replaced.

Code not found or not inserted

Request a new email code, check the selected mailbox and retry. Authenticator and SMS codes are separate. Unsupported fields can use Copy. Please do not post verification codes, tokens or passwords in public issues.
