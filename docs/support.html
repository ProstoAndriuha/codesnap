CodeSnap help

Gmail

Select Google / Gmail (OAuth) and authorize read-only Gmail access. Extra accounts use temporary sessions.

Yandex, Outlook, Yahoo, Mail.ru and other providers (100% local)

No servers or passwords are needed: choose your mail provider in the popup and click Open to keep its webmail tab open in Chrome. CodeSnap reads recent codes and activation links directly from the open tab. Fastmail also supports an optional local JMAP token and Outlook supports an optional local PKCE Client ID.

Activation links

When a website shows a "Check your email" confirmation screen, CodeSnap looks for safe HTTPS confirmation/magic links in recent emails and offers a click-to-open action after you verify the destination domain.

Unknown request after updating

Reload CodeSnap on chrome://extensions and reload any open webmail or verification tabs.
